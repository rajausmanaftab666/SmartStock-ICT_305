## Meetings
- Sprint planning: after class on the Wednesday each sprint starts (30 min).
- Stand-up: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 15 min: what I finished, what is next, any blockers.
- Sprint review and retrospective: in the buffer days before each assessment is due.
- Client check-in: once per sprint, run by the client liaison; minutes shared within 24 hours.

## Communication
- Day-to-day: team group chat. Reply within 12 hours on weekdays.
- Decisions and code discussion: GitHub issues and pull requests, so there is a record.
- Blocked for more than a day: say so at the next stand-up or in the chat straight away.

## Decision-making
- Technical decisions: anyone can propose; the Tech lead decides and records an ADR when the
  decision affects the architecture.
- Scope and priority: agreed with the client through the client liaison.
- Disagreements: majority vote; the role owner for that area breaks a tie.

## Ways of working
- Every change goes through a pull request with one approving review and passing CI.
- Each person commits from their own GitHub account.
- Card owners move their own cards: Backlog, In progress, In review, Done.

## Missed deadlines
1. Tell the team as early as possible.
2. The Scrum master re-plans the work at the next stand-up.
3. A second miss in the same sprint goes to the whole team. A continuing pattern is recorded
   with evidence for the Group Review Form.
